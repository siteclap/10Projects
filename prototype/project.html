Project Overview

Skyline Heights is a premium residential development by ABC Realty in Kharghar Sector 35, Navi Mumbai. The project offers thoughtfully designed 2 BHK and 3 BHK apartments with modern amenities and excellent connectivity.

Located just 1.2 km from Kharghar railway station and close to the Central Park, the project benefits from strong infrastructure — upcoming metro connectivity, established schools, and shopping centres.

Construction is currently at 40% (as of July 2026). ABC Realty has delivered 85% of their past projects on time, making this a moderate-risk investment with strong location fundamentals.
