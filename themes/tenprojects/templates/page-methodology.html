How the Fit Score Works

Every recommendation we make is backed by a transparent, 20-category scoring system. No hidden algorithms. No pay-to-rank. Here is exactly how it works.

The Fit Score in 60 Seconds

When you complete an assessment, our AI extracts your requirements — budget, location, configuration, lifestyle, timeline, and more. Then we score every project against those requirements across 20 distinct categories, each worth 0 to 100 points.

The final Fit Score is a weighted average of all 20 category scores. The weights change based on your buyer type (end-user vs investor) because different things matter to different buyers.

Result: your personal top 10 projects, ranked from best fit to tenth best fit, with a full breakdown of why each project scored the way it did.

Step 1: Hard Filters

Before scoring begins, we eliminate projects that simply cannot work for you. These are non-negotiable constraints:

  • Budget: Projects priced above your stated maximum budget are removed.
  • Configuration: If you need a 3 BHK and a project only offers 1 and 2 BHK, it is filtered out.
  • Location: Projects outside your selected locations/areas are excluded.
  • Possession: If you need possession within 12 months, under-construction projects with a 2027 delivery date are removed.

Only projects that pass all hard filters proceed to the scoring stage.

Step 2: The 20 Scoring Categories

Each project is scored from 0 to 100 in every category. Here is what each category measures:

1 Budget Fit
How well the project price aligns with your comfortable and maximum budget. Projects priced within your comfortable range score highest. Those near your max get a lower but passing score.
2 Location Fit
Whether the project is in your preferred location(s). Exact match scores 100. Adjacent or nearby locations get partial scores based on proximity.
3 Configuration Fit
Whether the project offers your desired BHK configuration. Exact match scores 100. Adjacent configs (e.g., you want 2 BHK, project has 2.5 BHK) score partially.
4 Carpet Area Fit
Whether the carpet area matches your needs. Larger families or those who specified minimum area requirements are scored against actual carpet area data.
5 Possession Fit
How well the expected possession timeline matches your needs. If you need immediate possession, ready-to-move projects score 100. Under-construction projects are penalised proportionally.
6 EMI Fit
Whether the estimated EMI is within your stated monthly budget. Calculated using standard 80% LTV, prevailing interest rates, and a 20-year tenure.
7 Commute Fit
Travel time from the project to your workplace or key daily destinations. Shorter commutes score higher. Considers road, rail, and metro connectivity.
8 Lifestyle Fit
Amenities and lifestyle preferences match. If you prioritise a gym, clubhouse, or garden, projects with those amenities score higher.
9 Developer Reliability
The developer's historical track record: on-time delivery rate, number of completed projects, financial stability, and customer satisfaction ratings.
10 Construction Stage
Current construction progress. Ready-to-move projects score higher for risk-averse buyers. New launches may score higher for investors seeking early-bird pricing.
11 Legal Confidence
RERA registration status, clear title verification, approved building plans, and environmental clearances. Fully compliant projects score 100.
12 Resale Liquidity
How easily you can resell the property. Based on location demand, transaction volume in the area, and developer brand value.
13 Rental Potential
Expected rental yield based on comparable rentals in the area, proximity to IT parks and offices, and overall rental demand.
14 Appreciation Drivers
Factors that drive future price appreciation: upcoming metro/highway, new commercial zones, government policy support, and historical price trends.
15 Risk Compatibility
Whether the project's risk profile matches your risk tolerance. New launches from small developers are higher risk. Ready-to-move from Tier 1 developers are lower risk.
16 Infrastructure Potential
Planned and under-construction infrastructure near the project: metro lines, expressways, airports, commercial hubs, and civic amenities.
17 Family Suitability
Proximity to good schools, hospitals, parks, and safety of the neighbourhood. Weighted higher for end-user buyers with families.
18 Urgency Match
How well the project's availability aligns with your buying timeline. Limited inventory projects that match your urgency score higher.
19 Inventory Availability
Whether your desired configuration is currently available. Sold-out configurations are penalised. Projects with good availability in your config score higher.
20 Proximity Score
Straight-line and travel distance to key landmarks you care about: workplace, family home, preferred school, airport, or railway station.

Step 3: Weight Profiles

Not all categories matter equally. A family buying their first home cares more about budget, location, and schools. An investor cares more about appreciation, rental yield, and resale liquidity.

We apply different weight profiles based on your buyer type:

End-User Weights

For families and individuals buying a home to live in.

  • Budget Fit — 15%
  • Location Fit — 12%
  • Possession Fit — 10%
  • Developer Reliability — 8%
  • Configuration Fit — 8%
  • EMI Fit — 8%
  • Commute Fit — 8%
  • Legal Confidence — 5%
  • Carpet Area Fit — 5%
  • Lifestyle Fit — 5%
  • Family Suitability — 5%
  • Proximity Score — 5%
  • Construction Stage — 3%
  • Risk Compatibility — 3%

Investor Weights

For buyers focused on returns, rental income, and capital gains.

  • Appreciation Drivers — 15%
  • Budget Fit — 12%
  • Rental Potential — 10%
  • Resale Liquidity — 10%
  • Location Fit — 8%
  • Developer Reliability — 8%
  • Infrastructure Potential — 8%
  • Legal Confidence — 7%
  • Risk Compatibility — 5%
  • Construction Stage — 5%
  • Possession Fit — 5%
  • Inventory Availability — 4%
  • Urgency Match — 3%

Step 4: AI-Generated Explanations

After scoring is complete, our AI generates a personalised explanation for each recommended project. This explanation:

  • Summarises why this project scored well for your specific requirements
  • Highlights the top 3 strengths based on your highest-weighted categories
  • Honestly flags any considerations or trade-offs
  • Is unique to every assessment — two different users see different explanations for the same project

The scoring itself is 100% deterministic — the same inputs always produce the same scores. AI is only used for the natural-language explanation layer.

How We Source and Verify Data

Accurate data is the foundation of accurate scoring. Here is how we collect and verify project information:

  • RERA filings: All project data is cross-referenced with MahaRERA public records.
  • Developer submissions: Developers provide pricing, floor plans, and construction updates directly.
  • On-ground verification: Our team visits projects to verify construction progress and amenities.
  • Market data: Price trends and transaction data from government registrations and industry databases.
  • Community input: Verified buyer reviews and feedback are incorporated over time.

Data Limitations

We strive for accuracy, but real estate data is inherently dynamic. Prices change, configurations sell out, and construction timelines shift. While we update data regularly, there may be brief periods where displayed information is outdated. Always verify critical details (pricing, availability, RERA status) directly with the developer before making a purchase decision.

Report an Inaccuracy

Found something wrong? We take data accuracy seriously. Use the "Report Inaccuracy" button on any project page, or email us at data@10projects.com with the project name and the specific issue. We investigate and correct errors within 48 hours.
